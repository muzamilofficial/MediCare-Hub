Millions of diabetic patients across Pakistan wish to fast during the holy month of **Ramadan**. With proper pre-Ramadan medical evaluation, tablet or insulin timing adjustments, and mindful Sehri and Iftar choices, many Type 2 diabetes patients can fast safely—while high-risk patients should understand when Islam grants an exemption to protect life and health.

### Does Checking Blood Sugar Break the Fast?

**No.** Religious scholars and medical boards agree that pricking your finger to check blood sugar on a home glucometer **does NOT invalidate your fast**. Checking your sugar at noon and 2 hours before Iftar is essential to prevent severe hypoglycemia (low sugar).

### When You MUST Break Your Fast Immediately

Never "push through" the last hour of a fast if your blood sugar reaches dangerous levels. Break your fast immediately if:

- **Blood sugar drops below 70 mg/dL** (hypoglycemia)—characterized by trembling hands, cold sweats, palpitations, dizziness, or blurred vision.
- **Blood sugar rises above 300 mg/dL** (hyperglycemia)—which carries a risk of severe dehydration and diabetic ketoacidosis.

### Smart Pakistani Sehri & Iftar Diet Guide

#### At Sehri (Pre-Dawn Meal)
- Eat as close to Fajr time as possible rather than at midnight.
- Choose **slow-digesting complex carbohydrates**: multigrain or whole-wheat *roti* (chapati), oats (*daliya*), boiled eggs, plain yogurt (*dahi*), and *daal* or lean chicken curry.
- Avoid oily parathas, white bread, and sweet tea, which spike sugar quickly and cause a crash by midday.

#### At Iftar (Evening Meal)
- Break your fast with **1 date (khajoor) and plain water**, then pray Maghrib before eating your main meal. This prevents sudden stomach overload and sugar spikes.
- Replace deep-fried pakoras, samosas, jalebi, and sugary red syrups with **black chana chaat, fruit chaat (without added sugar), grilled chicken tikka, and lemon water**.
- Drink 8 glasses of water gradually between Iftar and Sehri.

### Adjust Medicines 2 Weeks Before Ramadan

Never switch your morning and evening diabetes tablets or insulin doses on your own. Visit your diabetologist 2 to 4 weeks before Ramadan so your sulfonylurea or insulin units can be safely modified for Sehri and Iftar timings.
