Nearly **1 in 3 adults in Pakistan** over the age of 40 has high blood pressure (**Hypertension**), yet more than half do not know it because high BP often causes **zero symptoms** until it strains the heart, kidneys, or brain blood vessels.

### Myth vs. Fact: "I Only Take My BP Tablet When My Head Hurts"

One of the most common and dangerous mistakes we see in clinics is patients stopping their blood pressure medicine when they "feel fine" or taking a tablet only when they feel a headache or neck heaviness.

- **Fact:** Blood pressure tablets work by keeping your arteries relaxed 24 hours a day. Stopping them suddenly causes a **rebound spike in blood pressure**, which significantly increases the risk of stroke (*faalij*) or heart attack.

### Where Hidden Salt Hides in Pakistani Meals

To keep blood pressure below **130/80 mmHg**, limit total daily salt intake to less than one level teaspoon (5 grams) per day. Watch out for high-sodium items common on our dining tables:

- **Pickles (*Achaar*), chutneys with काला نمک (black salt), and papad**
- **Bakery rusks, salted biscuits, nimko, and samosas**
- **Packaged spice mixes (*masala* packets) and instant noodles**
- **Salted lassi and chaat masala sprinkled on fruit**

Instead, flavor home-cooked curries with fresh lemon juice, garlic (*lehsan*), ginger (*adrak*), coriander, roasted cumin (*zeera*), and black pepper.

### How to Measure Blood Pressure Accurately at Home

1. Sit quietly on a chair with back support and feet flat on the floor (do not cross your legs) for **5 minutes** before checking.
2. Avoid smoking, tea, coffee, or climbing stairs for 30 minutes beforehand.
3. Rest your arm on a table so the cuff is at **heart level**.
4. Record your readings in a notebook or phone app with the date and time, and bring that log to your doctor every 3 months.
