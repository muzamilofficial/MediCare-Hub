During May, June, and July, temperatures in Karachi, Interior Sindh, and Southern Punjab frequently cross **40°C to 46°C**, with high coastal humidity making the "feels-like" temperature even more dangerous. Elderly parents, young children, outdoor workers, and patients taking blood pressure medicines are at the highest risk of **heat exhaustion and heat stroke**.

### Understanding Heat Exhaustion vs. Heat Stroke

- **Heat Exhaustion:** Heavy sweating, weakness, muscle cramps, dizziness, headache, and nausea. This is an early warning that your body is losing water and salts rapidly.
- **Heat Stroke (Medical Emergency):** Body temperature rises above **103°F (39.5°C)**, skin becomes hot and dry (sweating may stop), pulse becomes rapid, and the person may become confused, slurred in speech, or unconscious.

### 6 Essential Habits During Pakistani Heatwaves

1. **Avoid Peak Sun Hours (11:00 AM to 4:00 PM):** Schedule market errands, bank visits, and non-urgent outings either early in the morning or after Asr prayers.
2. **Drink Before You Feel Thirsty:** Elderly people often lose their natural sense of thirst. Offer a glass of water, *kachi lassi* (diluted yogurt drink with a pinch of salt), lemon water (*shikanjabeen*), or ORS every 1 to 2 hours.
3. **Dress for Airflow:** Wear loose-fitting, light-colored cotton or lawn clothes. When stepping outside, cover your head with a wet cotton towel, cap, or umbrella.
4. **Cut Down on Tea and Soft Drinks:** Excessive hot chai and sugary carbonated sodas act as diuretics and can worsen dehydration.
5. **Cool the Body Regularly:** During load-shedding or humid afternoons, sponge the neck, armpits, and wrists with cool tap water or take a brief shower.
6. **Review Blood Pressure Medications:** Diuretic tablets ("water pills") used for high blood pressure can increase dehydration in extreme summer heat. Consult your physician before summer begins to see if your dose needs seasonal adjustment.

### First Aid If Someone Feels Faint from Heat

Immediately move the person into a shaded or air-conditioned room, loosen tight clothing, elevate their feet slightly, apply wet cloths to their forehead, neck, and underarms, and give small sips of ORS if they are fully awake.
